PROJECT DOCUMENTATION

Project Progress & Challenges

1. What have you done so far?

So far, we have created an interactive visualization that automatically progresses through the different years in our dataset, displaying a bar chart of video game sales across gaming platforms.

Users can filter the visualization by region and choose whether to display the top five or top ten platforms. We also added a dynamically updating informational section that highlights the leading platform and its estimated game sales for each year, making the story easier to follow.

Finally, we implemented playback speed controls so viewers can explore the progression of gaming platforms at their preferred pace.

2. What will be the most challenging part of your project to design and why?

We believe one of the most challenging parts of this project is designing dynamic x- and y-axes. Our dataset contains platforms with estimated game sales ranging from less than one million to over 170 million copies, making it difficult to maintain a consistent scale across years.

Additionally, some years contain only a few platforms, while others have enough to display all ten rankings. This creates challenges when adjusting the chart layout dynamically.

Another challenge is animating the bars themselves. Since we want to communicate changes in platform rankings over time, we aim to create smoother transitions that emphasize both changes in bar length and movement between rankings.

Source: Video Game Sales dataset (vgsales.csv). Sales are estimates in millions of copies. Values are grouped by game release year, not calendar-year purchases.
